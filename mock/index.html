Non-Binding Commercial Proposal

Welcome to SGS,

We appreciate the opportunity to present our commercial proposal (the "Proposal") for certification services in accordance with European Regulation 2017/745 for Medical Device and European Regulation 2017/746 for In Vitro Diagnostic device. This document outlines our non-binding fee proposal. Please note that the full scope of work for auditing, technical documentation assessment, and testing is not yet fully defined at the time of this Proposal's issuance.

In line with our commitment to be environmental responsible, we have included only essential information regarding our services and certification process. Each of our Proposals and any services described herein are subject to SGS General Conditions for Certification Services.

This Proposal is based on the information provided to us and has been prepared in accordance with the requirements of IAF MD 9 (International Accreditation Forum Mandatory Document 9). Details regarding justification of audit time are available on request. The audit time calculation includes inherent non-audit activities, such as, but not limited to, audit report preparation and writing.

This Proposal may be subject to revision should the information be updated or supplemented during the course of the assessment activities, particularly where such changes impact the scope or nature of the required assessment. This may result in additional or more extensive assessment activities.

This Proposal remains valid for a period of 60 days from date of issuance, or until the end of the current calendar year — whichever occurs first.
